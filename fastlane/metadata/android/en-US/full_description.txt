<h2>Description</h2>
<p>A manga app for E-Hentai, supporting Android &amp; iOS &amp; Windows &amp; MacOS &amp; Linux.</p>
<p>Still in development stage, welcome to submit issues or feature requests.</p>
<h2>Main Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>Mobile, tablet, desktop layout(3 kinds)</li>
<li>Vertical, horizontal, double column read page layout(4 kinds)</li>
<li>GalleryPage, Popular, Favorite, Watched, History, support multiple gallery list style</li>
<li>search, search suggestion, tap tag to search, file search, jump to a certain page</li>
<li>online reading and download, support restore download task, support synchronize updates after the uploader has uploaded a new version</li>
<li>archive download and automatic unpacking and reading</li>
<li>support loading local images and read</li>
<li>support assign priority to download task manually</li>
<li>support assign group to gallery and archive</li>
<li>favorite, rating, torrent, archive, statistics, share</li>
<li>password login, Cookie login, web login</li>
<li>support EX site(domain fronting optional)</li>
<li>vote for Tag, watch and hidden tags</li>
<li>comment, vote for comment</li>
<li>Fingerprint unlock (Removed in F-Droid builds due to non-free dependencies)</li>
</ul>
